ZERO DAY WOMEN

Women doing unprecedented things in cyber, security, and intelligence — and the stories that got them there.

From Dumb Ways To Get Hacked

Scroll

In cybersecurity, the most dangerous thing in the world is called a zero day.

It's a vulnerability nobody knew existed. There's no patch for it. No defence. No playbook. Nobody saw it coming.

And the thing about a zero day? By the time you realise it's there, it's already changed everything.

These women are zero days. And these are their stories.

About the show
This isn't a podcast about women trying
to make it in cyber

"These women already made it. They're already in the room. They're already doing the work. We're just finally telling those stories."

No empowerment speeches. No panels about leaning in. No cupcakes.

Zero Day Women tells the stories of women who built the thing, broke the thing, or found what nobody else was looking for — in cybersecurity, national security, intelligence, and the spaces in between.

Paths nobody had mapped. Backgrounds nobody expected. Work that matters and doesn't get enough airtime.

A new show from Dumb Ways To Get Hacked — same family, different side of the story.
