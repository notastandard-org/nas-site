Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 February 2026

Not A Standard Pty Ltd (acn: 686 284 042) ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit notastandard.org and its subdomains.

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it through our website forms. This includes:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, organisation (optional), and message content
  • Research subscribe form: your email address

We do not use cookies, analytics tracking, or advertising pixels on this website. We do not collect information automatically beyond standard server logs (see Hosting below).

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, send you research updates you have subscribed to, and communicate with you about our services. We will not use your information for any purpose other than what you provided it for without your consent.

Form processing

Our website forms are processed by Formspree, a third-party form handling service based in the United States. When you submit a form, your data is transmitted to and temporarily stored by Formspree before being forwarded to us via email. Formspree's privacy policy governs their handling of your data.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and referring page as part of standard server operations. This data is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Third-party disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information with anyone other than as described in this policy (Formspree for form processing, GitHub for hosting) unless required by law.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, and disclosure. Our website is served over HTTPS, and we use reputable third-party services with their own security practices.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and request corrections to any information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. To make a request, contact us at hello@notastandard.org.

If you have subscribed to our research updates, you can unsubscribe at any time by contacting us at the email address above.

Data retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are retained for the duration of any resulting business relationship. Subscription email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to make a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us at hello@notastandard.org.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
